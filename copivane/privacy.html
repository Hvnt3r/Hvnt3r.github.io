Copivane

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Copivane is a local-first macOS clipboard utility. We do not sell clipboard content, operate an advertising or analytics service, or upload clipboard history to a server.

Data processed on your Mac

When you turn capture on, Copivane processes clipboard representations, timestamps, inferred source-app metadata, previews, and—if enabled—on-device image OCR. Files remain references to their original locations and are not uploaded or copied into Copivane.

Local storage and protection

Clipboard payloads are encrypted locally with AES-GCM. Search previews and basic index metadata may remain unencrypted in the app's local sandbox database for fast search; sensitive-looking records avoid storing plaintext preview or OCR search text.

Your controls

You can pause capture, exclude apps or text patterns, blur sensitive-looking clips, choose automatic deletion, delete individual records, or erase all local history and its encryption key. Sensitive detection is heuristic and cannot guarantee that every secret is recognized.

Purchases

Apple processes Copivane Pro purchases. Copivane reads verified StoreKit entitlement status but does not receive or store payment details.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hvnt3r@foxmail.com.

Copivane

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 1 日

Copivane 是一款本地优先的 macOS 剪贴板工具。我们不会出售剪贴板内容，也不运营广告或分析服务，不会将剪贴板历史上传到服务器。

在本机处理的数据

打开记录后，Copivane 会在本机处理剪贴板内容、时间戳、推断的来源应用信息、预览，以及在启用后执行的本机图片 OCR。文件只保留原始位置引用，不会上传或复制进 Copivane。

本地存储与保护

剪贴板载荷在本机使用 AES-GCM 加密。为了快速搜索，应用本地沙盒数据库中的搜索预览和基础索引元数据可能未加密；疑似敏感记录不会保存明文预览或 OCR 搜索文字。

你的控制权

你可以暂停记录、排除应用或文字模式、模糊疑似敏感内容、设置自动删除、删除单条记录，或清除全部本地历史及其加密密钥。敏感检测是启发式的，不能保证识别所有秘密。

购买

Copivane Pro 的购买由 Apple 处理。Copivane 只读取经过验证的 StoreKit 权益状态，不接收或保存支付信息。

联系

如有隐私问题，请发送邮件至 hvnt3r@foxmail.com。
